Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your HuggyFamily Wallet account and the associated personal data directly in the app.

How to delete your account

  1. Open HuggyFamily Wallet and sign in.
  2. Go to the Family Selection screen.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Follow the on-screen instructions and confirm the deletion.

What happens to your data?

  • Your account and personal data, including personal accounts and their associated transactions, are deleted.
  • If other members remain in your family, shared family financial data is retained for those members.
  • If you are the last member of your family, the family and its associated data are also deleted.

When is the data deleted?

Account deletion is processed immediately after you confirm it in the app. There is no account recovery waiting period.

Some records may be retained separately by service providers where required by law or their applicable retention obligations, such as payment records managed by the app store.
